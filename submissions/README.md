## Week 2

* **`week2_TSA.ipynb`**  
  Jupyter notebook containing the solution for the Week 2 assignment.  

  **Topics covered:**  
  - Linear Regression  
  - Time Series Analysis (TSA)  

  **Additional Analysis:**  
  - Performed a **Pairs Trading analysis** between **EWA (Australia)** and **EWC (Canada)** ETFs  
  - Applied the principle of **mean reversion** in financial time series  
  - Tested for **stationarity** of the spread between the two indices

## Week 3

* **`week3.ipynb`**  
  Jupyter notebook containing implementations and experiments with **Kalman Filtering**.

  **Topics covered:**  
  - Kalman filter for **1D single-state estimation** (position only)  
  - Kalman filter for **two-state estimation** (position and velocity)  
  - Modeling process noise and measurement noise  
  - Study of the impact of **process noise covariance (Q)** and **measurement noise covariance (R)**  
  - Effect of time step on estimation performance  
  - **Tuning of Q and R** using error analysis to minimize estimation error  
  - Visualization of estimation error, Kalman gain, and convergence behavior
